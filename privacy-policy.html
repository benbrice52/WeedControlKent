Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Weed Control Kent is a service operated by Roundwood Solutions Ltd ("we", "us", "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use and protect information about you when you use this website or contact us about our services.

Roundwood Solutions Ltd is registered in England and Wales (Company No. 14984592). We are the data controller for the purposes of UK GDPR.

What information we collect

We only collect personal data when you choose to give it to us. This includes:

  • Contact form submissions — your email address, your phone number if you give it, and any details you include in your message about your site or requirements
  • Email and telephone enquiries — any personal data you include when contacting us directly

We do not collect any special category personal data. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide quotations for our services
  • Communicate with you about a contract or job if you become a client
  • Meet any legal or regulatory obligations

We will not use your personal data for marketing without your explicit consent, and we will not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

  • Legitimate interests — to respond to your enquiry and assess whether we can provide the service you need
  • Contract — to fulfil our obligations where a contract exists between us
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by law

Who we share your data with

We use Formspree to process contact form submissions. Your data is sent securely to Formspree's servers and then to our email inbox. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. We do not share your personal data with any other third parties except where required by law.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months in case you contact us again about the same or a related matter. Data relating to active or completed contracts is kept for 6 years in line with standard business record-keeping requirements. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Your rights below).

Cookies

This website uses the Google tag to run Google Ads conversion tracking. This measures how well our advertising works, for example whether someone who clicked one of our ads went on to send us an enquiry. We do not use Google Analytics or any other tracking.

The Google tag only sets cookies (such as _gcl_au) if you click Accept on our cookie banner. If you click Reject, no advertising cookies are set. Google may still receive limited information without cookies, such as the fact that a page was viewed, which it uses to estimate how well ads are performing.

We save your choice in your browser so we don't ask you again on every page. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at office@roundwoodsolutions.co.uk. We will respond within one calendar month.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
