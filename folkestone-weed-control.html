Why Certification Matters for Folkestone Commercial Sites

Commercial herbicide application is a legally regulated activity. All operatives must hold a minimum of PA1 certification, with PA6AW required for any work on hard surfaces, near drainage, or in proximity to watercourses. For Folkestone sites — many of which are near the coast, harbour drainage or the Royal Military Canal — PA6AW certification is particularly important.

Using an uncertified contractor creates liability for your business under the Control of Pesticides Regulations 1986. Weed Control Kent holds all required certifications and provides full documentation with every treatment.

  • PA1 certified — legal requirement for all commercial herbicide application
  • PA6AW certified — required for hard surfaces, car parks, areas near drains, watercourses and coastal features
  • BALI accredited — British Association of Landscape Industries, independently verified professional standards
  • £5 million public liability insurance — specifically covering herbicide application on commercial sites
  • Full documentation — COSHH assessments, risk method statements and written treatment records after every visit
