Why Rochester Businesses Choose BALI Accredited Contractors

Rochester has a significant public sector and institutional client base — schools, NHS facilities, and council-managed commercial properties — where contractor accreditation is often a procurement requirement. Our BALI accreditation means we meet these standards out of the box, with verified insurance, qualified operatives, and a formal code of professional conduct.
