What Our Commercial Weed Control Service Includes

Every commercial weed control engagement follows a consistent professional process. Whether you need a single treatment or an ongoing annual contract, the standards remain the same.

1

Free Site Survey

We visit your site to assess the areas requiring treatment, identify weed species present, note proximity to drainage, water features or sensitive areas, and produce an accurate written quote. No obligation.

2

COSHH Assessment & Risk Method Statement

Before any treatment is carried out, we produce site-specific documentation covering chemical selection, application method, PPE requirements, and controls for all identified risks including people, drainage, and wildlife.

3

Professional Herbicide Treatment

Treatment carried out by PA1/PA6AW certified operatives using calibrated professional spraying equipment. We select the appropriate herbicide product and application rate for the weed species, surface type and environmental conditions present.

4

Written Treatment Record

A full spraying record is completed for every treatment, including operative details, chemical used, dose rate, weather conditions, and environmental risk assessment. Copies provided to you for your own compliance records.

5

Seasonal Follow-Up (Contract Clients)

Most commercial sites benefit from 2–3 treatments per season. We operate on a scheduled basis, visiting at the optimal points in the growing season to prevent establishment and maintain consistent results throughout the year.

Why Certification Matters for Commercial Weed Control

Commercial herbicide application is a legally regulated activity. Any operative applying pesticides in a commercial context must hold a minimum of PA1 certification. For work on hard surfaces, near drainage, or in areas where spray drift could reach watercourses, PA6AW certification is also required.

These are not optional accreditations — they are legal requirements under the Control of Pesticides Regulations 1986. Commissioning weed control from an uncertified operator exposes your business to significant liability: if damage occurs to adjacent planting, drainage systems, or the environment, both the operative and the commissioning business can face enforcement action from the Health and Safety Executive or Environment Agency.

All Weed Control Kent operatives hold the required certifications. We carry full public liability insurance that specifically covers pesticide application, and our BALI accreditation provides independent verification of our professional standards. Every job comes with a complete paper trail.

Commercial Weed Control Contracts

For most commercial clients, a seasonal contract is more cost-effective than calling for reactive treatments once weeds are established. An annual contract typically includes:

  • A scheduled programme of 2–3 visits per growing season (typically March–October)
  • Pre-agreed pricing with no call-out charges between scheduled visits for covered areas
  • Treatment reports after every visit for your compliance records
  • A single point of contact and consistent operative where possible
  • Priority scheduling ahead of one-off clients during peak season

Contracts can cover a single site or a portfolio of properties across Kent. If you manage multiple sites, we can coordinate visit scheduling to align with your operational requirements.
